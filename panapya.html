Panapya

WIP WIP WIP WIP

Panapya is the sign language of Dagapya. It is currently a work in progress, but you can read its current documentation here.

Note that due to the limitations of VRChat, the facial expressions are unrelated to the signs, and the hand demonstrations are limited to the available hand gestures (but are still perfectly valid).

All signs are WIP
