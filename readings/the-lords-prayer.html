moypan Byamya,
teso e law tagyedin;
a maswe a kyu samu;
a kyu sogyadin i buli,
a kyu boti i gwesin,
boy daso buki boy tagyedin.
i dyo u moypan e moypan poluhan lano;
i sya bulu u moypan e moypan twadolusin;
buki a moypan i bulu e daw
teso i twadolusin u moypan.
i sya soy paka e moypan u tayu,
bemyu i twasepo e moypan doke twadolu.
oAmen.
Our Father,
who art in heaven,
hallowed be thy name;
thy kingdom come,
thy will be done,
on earth, as it is in heaven.
Give us this day our daily bread;
and forgive us our trespasses
as we have forgiven those
who trespass against us.
And lead us not into temptation,
but deliver us from evil.
Amen.
