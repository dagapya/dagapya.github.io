The Tower of Babel




< Back to Readings

1' may a ban daso i hali e min pya e twatoy moysamu.
2' sya saw a swe i potu doke damu, a swe i buli say kagaygosu law dego hoy oSinala, i gwedigyo boy sandin.
3' sya a swe i pya u toyswe e, "i buli; a moypan i sin e kokokado i banben tasyo e goy." sya a swe i hali e kokokado telu koko e oBitumen telu kokokadogano.
 
4' dyu a swe i pya e, "i buli; a moypan i dey telu moypan e hito e taseydey key pago law tagyedin; sya a moypan i sin e samu telu moypan; toybegu a moypan i dyu gwesili byo tela hoy ban daso."
5' Tagye i twapay buli telu loy e hito e taseydey, teso a twahawteydaw i myedey.
6' sya a Tagye i pya e, "i loy: a swe i swa e min hapudaw, a swe i hali e polu min pya; a pin i swa e dole sadimun doke teso a swe i dyu sin; soygoy teso a swe tegi sin i dyu mayben swa e twakobu boy swe.
7' i buli; a moypan i twapaypyu i sandin twamapi e swe pya; boysey a swe i soy mapi e pya hoy toyswe."
8' boysey a Tagye e swe i sili doke sandin byo tela hoy ban daso, sya a swe i tepi dey e hito.
9' boysey a dey i swa gwesamu e oBabele, boytaku boysandin a Tagye i twamapi e pya hoy ban daso, sya doke sandin a Tagye e swe i sili byo tela hoy ban daso.
1' Now the whole earth had one language and the same words.
2' And as they migrated from the east, they came upon a plain in the land of Shinar and settled there.
3' And they said to one another, "Come, let us make bricks and fire them thoroughly." And they had brick for stone and bitumen for mortar.
 
4' Then they said, "Come, let us build ourselves a city and a tower with its top in the heavens, and let us make a name for ourselves; otherwise we shall be scattered abroad upon the face of the whole earth."
5' The LORD came down to see the city and the tower, which mortals had built.
6' And the LORD said, "Look, they are one people, and they have all one language, and this is only the beginning of what they will do; nothing that they propose to do will now be impossible for them.
7' Come, let us go down and confuse their language there, so that they will not understand one another's speech."
8' So the LORD scattered them abroad from there over the face of all the earth, and they left off building the city.
9' Therefore it was called Babel, because there the LORD confused the language of all the earth, and from there the LORD scattered them abroad over the face of all the earth.
