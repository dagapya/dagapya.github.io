Dagapya is a minimalistic constructed language created by Vecderg in 2024, designed for ease of learning and flexibility in usage. It can be used as an international auxiliary language, a language for a fictional universe, or as a personal language for self-expression.

The grammar is very simple, fitting on a single piece of paper, and designed to be learnable by anyone on the planet. It also takes neutrality to the greatest extent, basing words on sound symbolism as a source of universality rather than taking words directly from any existing language.

Dagapya is NOT meant to replace or compete with any other language, instead using its small 800 word vocabulary as both a source of ease of learning and a level of simplicity to easily coexist with other languages.
