Dagapya Dictionary



> About

Dagapya is a constructed language created to be easily learned and spoken. Visit the official website for more information!

- Whole Word Search: Reads the search as a complete word (e.g. "cloth" will return "cloth" but not "clothing")
- Include Compound Words: Includes words that contain 2 or more root words
- Include Loanwords: Includes words that aren't part of the base vocabulary (country names, loanwords, letter names, etc.)

Note that in Dagapya, compound words are not always lexicalized (e.g. rail-vehicle just means rail-vehicle, but is listed as "train" because that's considered one of the most likely interpretations).
